    Your first class, start to finish

    1. Book any classFoundations is the gentlest start, but every class is open to beginners. Tell us about injuries when you book.
    2. Come 10 minutes earlyWe'll show you the cubbies, the water, and your reformer. Wear something fitted.
    3. Grab grip socksSocks with grips on the bottom keep you on the carriage. Your first pair is free.
    4. We set your springsYour instructor sets you up the first time, then shows you how to do it yourself.

    Questions people ask

    I've never done Pilates. Is that OK?

    Yes. About a third of every class is new to the reformer. Instructors give easier and harder versions of every move.

    What's the cancellation policy?

    Cancel up to 12 hours before for free. Later than that, or a no-show, uses the class (or a $15 fee on Unlimited).

    Can I come if I'm pregnant?

    Yes, after your doctor signs off. Start with a private session with June so she can adjust everything for you.

    Is there a shower?

    Two, with towels, shampoo and a hair dryer. Lockers are free. Bring a lock or borrow one.

    How is this different from mat Pilates?

    The reformer adds springs, so moves get lighter or heavier without weights. It's easier to start and harder to master.
