Questions owners ask

What does the monthly fee pay for?

Hosting, your domain, keeping the site secure, and changes whenever you need them: new prices, holiday hours, a new service, new photos. With Site + Booking it also covers the booking page and appointment reminders. Text or email the change and it's done.

How long until my site is live?

Usually about a week after you say yes. Most of that time is getting your details, photos, and hours right.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. If you want to stop, tell us and the monthly fee stops at the end of that month.

Can I start with Starter and add booking later?

Yes. You pay the difference in setup, $200, and your monthly fee moves to $50.

Do my customers pay anything to book?

No. Booking is free for your customers, and there are no per-booking fees for you.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes, same prices. We'll show you a mockup of the new version next to your current one first.

Can I get a discount?

Prices are the same for every business, so no one pays more than you. If budget is tight, the Starter Site is the place to begin, and you can add booking whenever you're ready.
