Most New York shops live on their regulars, and still lose new ones in small ways.

  • No website. People search for you and find someone else.
  • A site that breaks on a phone. Which is where nearly everyone looks.
  • Booking that's just "call us." At 11pm, nobody calls.
  • A missed call with no reply. By the time you call back, they've booked elsewhere.

A missed call still becomes a booking.

You're mid-cut and the phone rings. Instead of losing them, they get a text with your booking link, and book themselves.

That's one of the tools we add on top of your website, once it's live. Reminders and review requests work the same way: set up once, then they run on their own.

What we set up

  • A website that works

    Built for phones first. Your services, prices, hours, and location, easy to find. We keep it updated so you never log in to anything.

  • Online booking

    Customers pick a time without calling, any hour of the day.

  • Appointment reminders

    A text before every appointment, so fewer people forget or no-show.

  • Missed-call text-back

    A call you couldn't pick up still turns into a booking.

  • Review requests

    Sent after each visit, so happy customers help bring in new ones.
